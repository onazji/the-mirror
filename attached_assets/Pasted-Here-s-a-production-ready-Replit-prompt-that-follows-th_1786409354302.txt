## Rendering

Render the Light Behavior beneath the avatar.

Requirements:

- centered perfectly
- identical positioning across all states
- no clipping
- preserve transparency
- no bloom
- no glow filters
- no blur
- no additive blending
- use normal alpha compositing

The PNG should simply render behind the avatar.

---

## Styling

Keep opacity subtle.

Approximately:

```
opacity: 0.45–0.60
```

Adjust only if needed to preserve the avatar as the dominant element.

The Light Behavior should support the body—not compete with it.
