Mirror Information Modal — Physical Android Fit
Use the two attached physical-device photographs as the primary visual reference. This is a bounded responsive-layout correction to the existing Mirror informational modal.
The photographs reveal two related overflow problems that were not adequately visible in desktop/browser testing.
Horizontal problem
At the modal's normal leftmost position, the Energy axis and the first two matrix columns are visible, but the complete third column (Overdrive / Pressure / Anxiety) extends beyond the right edge of the viewport.
The user can horizontally scroll to reveal that column, but doing so clips content on the left. This modal should not behave as a horizontally scrollable chart.
The complete 3×3 matrix must be simultaneously visible:
Drift | Flow | Overdrive
Idle | Alignment | Pressure
Stagnant | Patience | Anxiety
Pace — Low / Steady / High must align directly with those three columns.
Energy — High / Steady / Low must align directly with the three rows.
Inspect the photographs carefully. The Energy/row-label region currently consumes or introduces more horizontal spacing than necessary. Compact that supporting axis region and allocate the available width primarily to the 3×3 matrix.
Prefer responsive grid sizing such as a compact axis column plus a minmax(0, 1fr) three-column matrix. Do not merely hide overflow.
Vertical problem
The closing sentence:
Your state shapes your thoughts, actions, and results.
is partially cut off at the bottom of the modal.
The intended mobile composition should ideally display, without vertical scrolling:
- The Mirror
- 4 Signals. One State.
- complete Pace/Energy 3×3 matrix
- Body visualization
- Mind visualization
- complete closing sentence
First recover vertical space by tightening excessive gaps, margins, and padding throughout the composition. Modestly reduce secondary/supporting typography only where necessary. Do not indiscriminately shrink the entire interface or materially reduce the prominence of the title, Body, Mind, or state information.
The closing sentence may use slightly smaller responsive typography if necessary, but it must remain comfortably readable and fully contained within the modal.
Acceptance criteria on narrow mobile
- zero horizontal scrolling;
- complete 3×3 matrix visible simultaneously;
- all three Pace labels aligned with their columns;
- all three Energy labels aligned with their rows;
- Body fully visible;
- Mind fully visible;
- closing sentence fully visible;
- no content clipped by the modal boundaries;
- preserve the existing terminology, conceptual relationships, visual identity, and desktop behavior.
Do not modify the state model, rename states, redesign the artifact toolbar, or implement the future monetizable feature.
Run existing tests and production build afterward and report exact files changed and the responsive-layout strategy used.
