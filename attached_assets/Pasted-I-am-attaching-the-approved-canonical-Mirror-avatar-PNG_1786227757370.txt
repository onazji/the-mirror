About Mirror Avatars
Mirror avatars are behavioral silhouettes, not characters.
Psychological state is conveyed through posture, weight distribution, tension, orientation, and hand placement.
Identity markers—including facial features, clothing, ethnicity, hairstyle, age, and accessories—are intentionally omitted so that attention remains on the state itself rather than the individual.
Your selection simply chooses which symbolic form appears during Mirror card reveals.
Both variants communicate the exact same psychological state.
Future emotional nuance is expressed through the Mirror Light System rather than changes to the body itself.
